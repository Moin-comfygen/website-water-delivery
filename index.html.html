Help & FAQs

How do I place an order?
You can order directly from the AQZO Customer App. Select your product, quantity, and delivery schedule. Or you can contact your Salesman to place an order on your behalf.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit (with monthly billing), and Online Payments (UPI, Wallet, Cards) via the app.
How does the jar balance work?
When you receive filled jars, your balance increases. When you return empty jars, your balance decreases. You can track your balance in the app. A security deposit per jar may apply, refundable upon return.
Can I change my subscription schedule?
Yes, you can modify your schedule anytime via the app — Daily, Alternate Days, Weekly, Monthly, or Custom Days.
What if I'm outside my branch's service area?
Your order will be routed through our Distributor network. Super Admin will assign a Distributor to fulfill your order.
