How It Works

From order to delivery — a seamless, transparent process designed for your convenience.

01

Place Order

Order via the AQZO Customer App or through your Salesman. Choose your schedule and quantity.

02

Confirmed & Prepared

Your branch confirms the order, checks inventory, and prepares your fresh, hygienic 20L jars.

03

Dispatched for Delivery

A Delivery Partner is assigned. You get real-time tracking and an estimated arrival time.

04

Delivered & Collected

Receive your order, make a seamless payment (COD/Credit/Online), and return your empty jars.
